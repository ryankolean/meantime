About

The meantime is the good part.

The stretch between whatever you just did and whatever you have to do next. One drink or six. We built a bar for that.

How we got here

Coffee in California, beer in Detroit.

Erik and Elliot met working coffee in California more than a decade ago and both ended up in breweries. Elliot and Brittney moved to Detroit first. Erik followed during the pandemic, sight unseen, which tells you something about how the three of them make decisions.

Between them they have spent years behind Detroit bars: Eastern Market Brewing, Florian East, Cøllect. In May 2025 they opened Meantime in the old 8 Degrees Plato space on Cass, and started pouring.

The room is mid-century and deliberately unfussy. Striped sofa, cut flowers, low tables, good light. Nobody here is going to be precious at you about beer.

The beer

Two side pulls, pouring Czech lagers the way they're meant to be poured.

A LUKR side-pull faucet lets you control the pour and the foam instead of fighting both. It is why a pilsner here tastes softer and reads sweeter than the same beer poured straight.

It also makes the mlíko possible: a glass of nothing but dense foam, meant to be drunk in one go. It sounds like a stunt. It is not. Buy a foamy for your homie.

  • Midwest Leisure, our Czech pilsner with Florian East, is the house pour
  • The rest of the list moves constantly: local, national, sour, dark, cider, seltzer
  • Full or half pours, so you can try two
  • A retail fridge for taking something home

House rules, loosely

Ask the 8 ball. It decides.

There is a magic 8 ball behind the bar. Shake it, and it picks your shot: house, fancy, or Henny. It has never once been wrong, which is either a sign of good bartending or low standards.
