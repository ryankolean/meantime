## Identity

- **What it is:** a neighborhood **lager bar** at 3409 Cass Ave, Detroit, MI 48201, in the Cass Corridor / Midtown, in the former 8 Degrees Plato space.
- **Opened:** May 2025, after two soft-opening weekends.
- **Owners:** **Erik Annonson**, **Brittney Carnahan**, **Elliot Hoffman**. Annonson and Hoffman met in the California coffee industry more than a decade ago and both moved into brewing. Hoffman and Carnahan moved to Detroit first; Annonson followed during the pandemic, "sight unseen."
- **Pedigree:** Hoffman came from Eastern Market Brewing Company and Florian East. Annonson came from Cøllect Beer Bar. This is an operator-run bar with deep Detroit beer-scene roots, and the site should read that way.
- **Stated goal, in their words:** "Our goal is that we have a really fervent community of people who come on a regular basis and feel like, 'this is where I hang out.'" (Hoffman) and "A place for people to come together and create communities, be a part of a culture." (Annonson)
- **Name meaning:** the meantime is the nebulous stretch where you can have one drink or stay for six. The site tagline states it: **"Life is what happens while you're in the meantime."**
- **Hours (live site, 2026-09-16):** Mon 3-10, Tue closed, Wed/Thu 3-10, Fri 3-11, Sat 12-11, Sun 12-10. Press from opening said Thursday to Monday, so they have added days since launch.
- **Phone:** aggregators list **408-504-4770**. Unverified, and a California area code, which is consistent with the owners' history but needs owner confirmation before it goes on a page.

## Program

- **Czech side pull.** Two LUKR side-pull taps for Czech lagers and pilsners. This is the bar's signature piece of equipment and shows up everywhere: tap list entries read "on LUKR", the wifi password on the printed menu is `LukrRules`, and the menu sells a **mlíko shot** ("all foam that drinks like a cloud - meant to be chugged in one gulp / buy a foamy for your homie").
- **House flagship:** **Midwest Leisure**, a Czech pilsner collaboration with Florian East. Confirmed live on the current tap list.
- **Breadth, not snobbery.** The observed tap and retail list spans Czech pilsner, Japanese rice lager, doppelbock, NEIPA, imperial stout, smoothie sour, cider, hard kombucha, seltzer, barleywine, and Belgian IPA, from Detroit-area brewers (Drafting Table, Urbanrest, Florian East, Pux) alongside national names (Sierra Nevada, Prairie, Bottle Logic, Grimm, De Ranke).
- **Retail fridge** for on-site or to-go, plus non-alcoholic options.
- **No kitchen.** Guests are welcome to bring their own food, and food pop-ups are part of the plan. A visitor account mentions eating bao there, which reads as a pop-up rather than a house menu.
- **Pal membership.** Announced at launch: $80 the first year, $60 annually after, for 15% off in-house, 25% off Mondays, a $5 house lager, a merch discount, and three parties a year. Sign-up was framed as "through Memorial Day" in 2025, so current status and pricing must be confirmed. **It is completely absent from the website**, which is the single largest content gap.
- **Magic 8 ball** behind the bar picks a mystery drink. It is on the printed menu as "MAGIC 8 BALL SHOT / shake your fate / HOUSE / FANCY / HENNY" and it is the most repeatable piece of brand theatre they have.

## Visual system (from logo files and printed menu)

- **Wordmark:** `meantime`, all lowercase, in a heavy geometric display face with angular shear cuts on the m, t and e and a dot-free tittle treatment. Distinctive, retro-modern, friendly rather than precious. The typeface has not been identified and is not one of the two webfonts on the site. **Source file needed.**
- **Mark:** a square built from a yellow triangle, a pink triangle, and a dark triangle that read together as an **M**, or as a folded letter. Works at small sizes; already used as the favicon-scale element and printed as a divider on the menu above the wifi note.
- **Palette in the brand assets:** ink `#1A120C`, yellow `#FDD757`, pink `#FBC9D8`, on white or warm off-white. Three colors, high energy, no gradients.
- **Printed menu** is set in a different world from the website: bold condensed sans for item names, an italic serif for the jokes underneath, hairline rules and boxes, letter-spaced small-caps section labels, all in one ink on white. It is a diner placemat by way of a zine, and it is the most on-brand artifact the business has produced.
- **Room, per press and the one site photo:** mid-century modern, deliberately not high-brow. Striped vintage sofa, cut flowers, low table, clean but cozy.

## Voice

The printed menu is the voice document. The banner line is:

> **WE'RE GLAD YOU'RE HERE // LAUGH TOO LOUD // STAY FOR ANOTHER**

Item copy is dry, Midwestern, and self-aware:

- BANQUET, $4: "dad beer - can't beat a stubby"
- MIDWEST LEISURE & OG-D, $10: "ope, just gonna sneak past ya"
- MARGARITA, $12: "Shaken, this ain't Chili's babe"
- SPAGHETT WESTERN, $8: "Arrivederci Cowboy"
- MALÖRT, $9: "Tonight's the night"
- CAPRI SUN, $2: "kids these days"
- MIXED DRINKS, $10-14: "if we have it & know it, it's yours / what'll it be?"
- Non-alcoholic sidebar, set vertically: "BE A PAL & FLIP ME OVER"

Rules that fall out of this: lowercase-friendly, short, never explains the joke, never uses beer-nerd vocabulary to establish authority, and treats the reader as a regular who is already in on it. The website currently has none of this voice on it.

## Gap: Instagram and the printed menu versus the website

The brand exists in three places and the website is the weakest of them. The logo, the menu artwork, the voice, the Czech program, the membership, the owners, and the room are all real and none of them are on the site beyond a header PNG, one photo, and a quote. The rebuild is mostly a transfer problem, not an invention problem.
