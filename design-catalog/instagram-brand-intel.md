## Voice, from real captions

The feed writes exactly the way the printed menu does. Verbatim:

> "Friday Jr. at your neighborhood hang. 3-10."

> "Another great weekend in the books with the best people. Lions won too. It's a new week to introduce somebody to a mlíko. 3-10 over here on Cass."

> "Nah like, these guys their whole thing is Foam. Every one I take there I make them do a Foamy. You're not allowed to order an IPA you can ONLY ORDER LAGERS AND FOAM SHOTS. And ranch waters. 3-11."

> "You just never know what could happen at the ol' bar and grill. 3-10. PS there is absolutely no grill we do not have a kitchen."

> "One million options for your Sunday sports party and we're doin' the song and dance too. You know all your favorite people are here though. Kickoff at 3. 'Bergs at anytime. There's no such thing as regret from Underbergs, Mike."

Patterns that hold across the sample:

1. **Almost every caption ends with the day's hours.** "3-10." "3-11." "12-10." It is the sign-off, and it is why the hours block belongs on every page.
2. Second person, in on the joke, no beer-nerd credentialing.
3. They undercut themselves on purpose ("there is absolutely no grill we do not have a kitchen").
4. Regulars get named in captions. The bar is a set of people, not a product.
5. Photo credits are always given as "📸: @name".
